Summary: Civil Construction Suite is an ad-free, standalone civil engineering calculation & utility application. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal user data or device identifiers to external servers.

1. Overview

Civil Construction Suite is published and maintained by Buildex, owned and operated by Moawia Husnain. This Privacy Policy explains our data privacy practices when you use our Android application.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Civil Construction Suite operates completely offline and locally on your device:

  • Personal Data: We do not collect, request, or store any personal data such as your name, phone number, email address, or location.
  • Device & Usage Data: We do not track or store advertising IDs, IP addresses, hardware identifiers, or usage analytics.
  • Engineering Calculations: All inputs, structural parameters, BOQ estimates, diary logs, and unit conversion data remain strictly local to your device's private app storage.

3. Permissions Used by the App

The app requires minimal Android permissions solely for executing core features requested directly by the user:

  • Local Storage (Exporting PDF/Images): Used exclusively when you choose to export BOQ invoices, graphs, or calculation reports to your device storage.
  • Sensors (Spirit Level & Compass): Uses device orientation sensors locally to display real-time level angles and cardinal directions. No sensor data is ever stored or transmitted.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Civil Construction Suite contains NO advertisements, NO third-party tracking SDKs, and NO external analytics tools. We do not use Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, or any tracking frameworks.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for general audiences and professionals, and does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

6. Security

We value your privacy and security. Because all data processed by the app is handled entirely on your local device, your calculations and data remain 100% private and secure under your control.
